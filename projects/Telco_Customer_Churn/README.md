# Telco Customer Churn

## Problem

### Description

Customer churn occurs when customers discontinue their subscriptions or leave a telecommunications company. Predicting churn helps businesses understand customer behavior, identify potential losses, and improve customer retention.

The objective is to predict whether a customer will leave the company based on demographic, service, and account information.

### Solution

Develop a Machine Learning classification model to predict customer churn.

The solution involves:

- Data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.
- Feature engineering and selection.
- Training and comparing classification models.
- Evaluating performance using Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC.

The goal is to identify customers at risk of leaving and help businesses improve retention strategies.
