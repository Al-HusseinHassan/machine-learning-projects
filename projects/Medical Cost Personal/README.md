# Medical Cost Personal

## Problem

### Description

Medical insurance costs vary depending on personal characteristics and lifestyle factors such as age, BMI, smoking status, and family size.

The objective is to understand the factors affecting medical insurance charges and predict individual insurance costs based on personal information.

### Solution

Develop a Machine Learning Regression model to predict medical insurance charges.

The solution involves:

- Data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.
- Feature engineering and selection.
- Training and comparing regression models.
- Evaluating performance using MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R².

The goal is to estimate insurance costs and identify the factors that contribute most to medical expenses.

## Insights

### Features

`age | sex | bmi | children | smoker | region`

### Target

`charges`
